# Synthex AI

## Project Overview

**Synthex AI** is an AI-powered interview preparation and skill enhancement platform designed to help students, fresh graduates, and professionals prepare effectively for technical interviews. The platform conducts AI-generated mock interviews, evaluates user responses, identifies weak areas, recommends personalized learning resources, and provides targeted practice sessions to improve interview performance.

---

# Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers struggle to prepare effectively for technical interviews due to the lack of personalized guidance and intelligent feedback. Existing interview preparation platforms primarily provide static question banks and generic assessments, making it difficult for users to identify their actual weaknesses and improve efficiently.

There is a need for an intelligent platform that can analyze interview performance, detect skill gaps, provide personalized recommendations, and offer targeted practice to enhance users' interview readiness.

---

# Proposed Solution

Synthex AI addresses this problem by providing an AI-powered interview preparation platform that simulates real interview experiences. The system generates interview questions based on the user's selected role, evaluates responses using AI, identifies weak technical concepts, and recommends learning resources and practice sessions tailored to the user's performance.

This personalized approach enables users to continuously improve their interview skills and increase their chances of success in technical interviews.

---

# Project Goals

The primary goal of Synthex AI is to build an intelligent interview preparation platform that helps users improve their technical interview skills through AI-driven analysis, personalized feedback, and targeted learning recommendations.
