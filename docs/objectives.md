# Project Objectives

## Overview

The objectives of **Synthex AI** are to develop an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps users improve their technical interview skills through intelligent analysis, personalized feedback, and targeted learning recommendations.

---

# Primary Objective

To design and develop an AI-powered interview preparation web application that enables users to practice mock interviews, receive AI-generated feedback, identify weak areas, and improve their interview performance through personalized learning and targeted practice.

---

# Specific Objectives

## 1. User Authentication

- Develop a secure user authentication system.
- Allow users to register, log in, and manage their profiles.

---

## 2. AI Interview Generation

- Generate interview questions based on the selected job role.
- Support multiple technologies and interview domains.
- Provide different difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard).

---

## 3. AI Answer Evaluation

- Evaluate user responses using Artificial Intelligence.
- Assign performance scores.
- Provide detailed feedback and improvement suggestions.

---

## 4. Weakness Detection

- Analyze interview responses.
- Detect weak technical concepts.
- Identify recurring mistakes.
- Generate a weakness analysis report.

---

## 5. Personalized Learning Recommendations

- Recommend learning resources based on weak topics.
- Suggest documentation, tutorials, and practice materials.
- Create personalized learning paths.

---

## 6. Target Practice

- Generate practice questions focused on weak areas.
- Help users strengthen technical knowledge.
- Measure improvement after each practice session.

---

## 7. Progress Tracking

- Maintain interview history.
- Display performance statistics.
- Compare previous interview scores.
- Track learning progress over time.

---

## 8. User Experience

- Develop a responsive and user-friendly interface.
- Ensure smooth navigation across all modules.
- Provide an intuitive interview experience.

---

# Expected Outcomes

After completing the project, the platform will enable users to:

- Practice AI-powered mock interviews.
- Receive personalized interview feedback.
- Identify technical weaknesses.
- Improve knowledge through targeted practice.
- Track interview preparation progress.
- Build confidence for real technical interviews.

---

# Project Vision

The vision of **Synthex AI** is to create an intelligent interview preparation platform that goes beyond traditional question banks by providing personalized AI-driven guidance, helping users continuously improve their technical skills and increase their chances of success in job interviews.

---

# Conclusion

The objectives of Synthex AI focus on combining Artificial Intelligence with modern web technologies to create a personalized, efficient, and data-driven interview preparation platform. The project aims to make interview preparation smarter, more interactive, and more effective for students, fresh graduates, and professionals.
